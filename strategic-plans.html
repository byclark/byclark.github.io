Strategic Plans in Public Affairs & Planning

A growing collection of strategic plans, dean's profiles, and DEI plans from schools of public affairs, public policy, and urban planning. The library aims to make peer institutions' planning documents easier to find, compare, and learn from — for deans, faculty, students, and accreditation teams.

Each entry links to the original document. Coverage skews toward NASPAA and ACSP member programs. ← Back to Tools

Strategic Plan 2026–2029
University of Oregon
School of Planning, Public Policy and Management (PPPM)
PPPM Strategic Plan

Approved June 4, 2026. Organized around a central question — how PPPM can leverage engaged research and community to deliver positive social impact for Oregon and beyond — the plan sets four strategic priorities (Student Learning and Success; Alumni and Career Engagement; Research for Positive Social Impact; and Communication and Visibility), 11 goals, and concrete action steps, informed by a review of 24 peer programs and aligned with the university's Oregon Rising plan.

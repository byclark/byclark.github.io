Graduate Program Cost Comparison

Compare the total estimated cost of UO PPPM graduate programs — MPA, MURP, MS, and MPP — against peer programs nationwide. Accounts for in-state vs. out-of-state tuition, WICHE WRGP eligibility, and living expenses, so you can make an apples-to-apples comparison.

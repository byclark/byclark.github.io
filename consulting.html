Consulting Services

Financial Health & Budget Analysis

Data Analytics & Program Evaluation

Program outcome evaluation Survey design & analysis 311 & service request data analysis IoT & sensor data interpretation

Coproduction strategy & design Organizational effectiveness review Citizen engagement system design Strategic planning facilitation
